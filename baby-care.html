Questions & Answers

New parents, we've heard it all — ask away

Do attendants have newborn-specific experience?
Yes. Our baby care attendants are specifically trained and experienced in newborn and infant care — not just general child care. They understand feeding patterns, colic management, safe bathing, and the critical routines of the first few weeks of a baby's life.
Is postnatal mother support included?
Yes, particularly with the 24-hour plan. Our attendants provide gentle guidance and practical support to the mother — helping with feeding posture, postpartum rest, and the emotional adjustment of early motherhood. This is something many families find just as valuable as the newborn care itself.
Can I switch from 12hr to 24hr plan?
Absolutely. Many families start with the 12-hour plan and upgrade once they see the benefit of continuous support. Just tell us and we'll adjust without any disruption to the attendant or routine.
What if I'm not satisfied with the attendant?
We have a 24-hour replacement guarantee. If for any reason the attendant is not the right fit — personality, approach, or skill — just let us know and we will send a replacement within 24 hours. Your baby's comfort and your peace of mind matter most.
Do you serve areas outside central Bengaluru?
We cover all major areas across Bengaluru — from Whitefield and HSR Layout to Koramangala, Electronic City, Hebbal, and more. If you're unsure about your area, WhatsApp or call us and we'll confirm within minutes.

Have questions? Talk to our Care Managers directly.

Our care managers pick up. No automated menus, no queue. Just a real conversation about your family's needs.
