Questions & Answers

For complex cases, families want clarity

What kind of cases do you handle?
Our nursing service handles ICU step-down cases, ventilator-dependent patients, post-surgical high-monitoring care, palliative and end-of-life support, neurological conditions, and complex bedridden cases. If you're not sure whether your case qualifies, call us — we do a case assessment before committing.
Is a case assessment required before booking?
For complex cases, yes — a brief case assessment helps us send the right nurse for your specific needs. This is done over a phone or WhatsApp call with our clinical coordinator and usually takes 15–20 minutes. It ensures you get qualified staff matched to the actual situation.
Can nursing staff administer injections and IV?
Yes. Our qualified nurses are trained and experienced in both injection administration and IV management. All clinical procedures are carried out per the treating physician's prescriptions and protocols.
How is this different from a Nursing Assistant?
A Nursing Assistant provides trained support — vitals, hygiene, medication reminders, bedside care. Our Nursing Service involves qualified nurses with clinical training who can handle more complex procedures, higher dependency patients, and medically supervised care plans. Think of it as the step up when the situation demands it.
Do you serve all areas in Bengaluru?
Yes, we serve all major areas across Bengaluru. For complex nursing service cases, please call or WhatsApp us directly so we can confirm availability and match the right qualified nurse for your location and care profile.
