Questions & Answers

What families want to know

Can Nursing Assistants give injections?
Nursing assistants can provide medication support including some injection administration under proper medical direction. For complex clinical procedures like IV therapy, our Nursing Service team is the right fit. If you're unsure, call us and we'll assess your case honestly.
Do you coordinate with our family doctor?
Yes. Our nursing assistants follow the treating doctor's care plan and communicate any changes in the patient's condition to the family promptly. We can also relay relevant updates to your physician if required. Open communication is part of how we work.
What conditions do you support?
Our nursing assistants regularly support patients recovering from surgery, elderly patients who are bedridden or semi-mobile, and those managing chronic conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and COPD. If your case is more complex (ICU step-down, ventilator-dependent), our Nursing Service team may be more appropriate.
Can I upgrade to Nursing Service later?
Absolutely. Many families start with a nursing assistant and upgrade as care needs evolve. Just speak to our care manager — the transition is handled smoothly with a proper handover so care is never interrupted.
What if we're not happy with the nursing assistant?
Just call or WhatsApp us. We have a 24-hour replacement guarantee — if you're not satisfied for any reason, we will send a replacement within 24 hours. No questions, no delays.

Have questions? Talk to our Care Managers.

Our care managers pick up. No automated menus, no queue. Just a real conversation about your family's needs.
