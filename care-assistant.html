Questions & Answers

Things families often ask us

What's the difference between a Care Assistant and a Nursing Assistant?
A Care Assistant handles daily personal care — bathing, hygiene, mobility, companionship, and meals. They don't perform medical procedures. A Nursing Assistant has additional clinical training for vitals monitoring, medication support, and basic wound care. If you're unsure which fits your situation, just call us — we'll guide you honestly.
Can I request the same assistant every day?
Yes. Consistency matters in care, and we know your loved one is most comfortable with someone familiar. We do our best to send the same assistant regularly. If a change is needed, we'll always inform you in advance and do a proper handover.
Is night shift available?
Yes, we offer both day and night 12-hour shifts. Just tell us your preference when you reach out. Our 24-hour plan provides continuous round-the-clock support without shift gaps, which many families find more convenient and cost-effective.
What if I need help only a few days a week?
Completely fine. We work around your schedule — whether it's 3 days a week, weekdays only, or a short-term arrangement. There's no rigid minimum. Just tell us what you need and we'll find a way to make it work.
Do you serve areas outside central Bengaluru?
We cover all major areas across Bengaluru — Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Indiranagar, Electronic City, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, and many more. If you're unsure about your area, WhatsApp or call us and we'll confirm within minutes.
